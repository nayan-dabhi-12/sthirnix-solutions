Microsoft Dynamics 365 Modules We Deploy

We configure and customize Microsoft's industry-leading business applications to solve the exact operational bottlenecks inside your specific departments.

Sales

Empower your sales team to close deals faster and build stronger relationships. We configure the Sales module to give you a 360-degree view of your pipeline, automating daily tasks and using AI to predict exactly which leads will convert into revenue.

Core Features

  • Lead & Opportunity Management
  • Sales Pipeline Automation
  • Quotes & Orders
  • Forecasting & Sales Analytics
  • AI-Powered Sales Insights

Customer Service

Turn your support center into a competitive advantage. We equip your service agents with unified workspaces that allow them to resolve complex issues instantly across multiple channels, while giving your customers the power of intelligent self-service.

Core Features

  • Case Management
  • SLA & Escalation Management
  • Knowledge Base Architecture
  • Omnichannel Customer Support
  • Customer Self-Service Portals

Field Service

Deliver seamless, proactive on-site support. We optimize the Field Service module to automatically schedule the right technicians, track expensive assets in real-time, and shift your operations from reactive repairs to predictive maintenance.

Core Features

  • Work Order Management
  • Intelligent Resource Scheduling
  • Mobile Field Service Applications
  • Asset Management
  • IoT Predictive Maintenance

Customer Insights & Marketing

Stop guessing what your customers want. We unify your fragmented customer data to build highly personalized, automated marketing campaigns that trigger dynamically based on real-time customer behavior and engagement.

Core Features

  • Automated Customer Journeys
  • Targeted Email Marketing
  • Intelligent Lead Nurturing
  • Dynamic Customer Segmentation
  • Event Management

Project Operations

Bridge the gap between project delivery and financial profitability. We deploy Project Operations to help your team manage resource bandwidth, track billable hours accurately, and ensure every enterprise project is delivered on time and strictly under budget.
